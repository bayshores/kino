# kino feature ledger

Every kino feature, whether it is proven, and the proof. Agents edit through ten MCP tools and the
`kino` command line; the app is a dashboard ([docs/agent-first-plan.md](docs/agent-first-plan.md)).
Each row must show that an agent can reach the feature and that the render is right (AGENTS.md).

How to read it:

- **Verified**: a test named in the row exercises the agent path or engine and checks the result.
- **Partly verified**: some cases are tested; the row says which are not.
- **Not verified**: built, but no test or real run proves it yet.
- **Not built**: a known gap.
- Numbers are from an Apple M3 Pro (11 cores, 18 GB) unless the row says otherwise; times taken
  while other jobs ran vary by about 10%.
- Test files are paths from the repo root.

## Open items at a glance

- **Reachability**: 27 of the 93 audit rows are still open (16 PARTIAL, 11 UI/NATIVE); VoiceOver
  never checked. See [Reachability audit](#reachability-audit).
- **Live model runs**: the direction, storyboard and build-order evals have never run with a real
  model (no `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY`). Codex has not run the A4 brand-kit gate.
- **Real sessions**: no real Claude Code session watched answering a brief, stop or storyboard from
  the phone (needs a person).
- **Crew**: run only with stub programs, never the real `claude` or `codex`.
- **Mac app**: not notarized (ad-hoc signature), no Intel build, never run on a fresh Mac; menus not
  clicked by hand since the editor was removed.
- **Real footage**: cut-out, flow interpolation and shot detection tested on generated clips and one
  3D trailer, not real camera footage or drawn animation.
- **By ear**: sound features (ducking, voice isolation, speech cuts, limiter) are measured, never
  listened to (by rule, no audio is played).
- **Dashboard settings not built**: brand kits, check strictness, platform presets, history retention.

## Quality checker

- **What it runs**: Verified. `check` runs `engine/quality/`: 104 rules, 26 gates (plus signature_missing
  on a final render) and render checks, each finding with `measured`, `required` and a fix; one of 2 samples or fewer is only `info`. Evidence:
  `engine/qualityRules.test.js` (every rule fires on its bad example only), `backend/quality-pixels.test.mjs`.
- **Polish rules**: Verified on a real trailer project: font_fallback, text_collision, text_wrap and six more
  (`engine/quality/rules/polish.js`) flag 12 of 14 target defects with no new errors on 330 kino scenes;
  all but render_artifact and small_on_empty (a hint now) have a proven `check {fix:"auto"}` fix; held_static pushes a still end card over
  3 s and fails one over 4.5 s, a slow push alone counting as still. Evidence: `engine/qualityPolish.test.js`,
  `backend/quality-polish-pixels.test.mjs`, `backend/agent-polish-fix.test.mjs`.
- **Ghosted text** (`text_ghost`, gate): Verified. Flags one text dissolving into another in place
  (it caught a real first pass, none of 390 recipe scenes); the auto fix waits for the old text
  to leave. Evidence: `engine/qualityPolish.test.js`, `engine/sequence.test.js`.
- **Screen rules**: Partly verified. frozen_screen and screen_unreadable read import-time picture facts
  (`engine/quality/rules/screens.js`) and auto fix with a push into the busy part; small screenshots in
  feature-callout stay a hint. Evidence: `engine/qualityPolish.test.js`, `engine/blocks.test.js` (screens).
- **Edit from footage** (`compose {footage}`): Partly verified. One call cuts a real project's clips and song
  into a 0-error edit with captions, title, end card and ducked music; a few warnings remain (loud
  accents over budget), and a
  talking-head proof, seed comparison and agent test are still to do. Evidence: `engine/footage.test.js`.
- **Music-led pacing** (`compose {footage, pace, accents}`): Partly verified. Runs of whole beats on
  the song's grid, calm open, fast ending and downbeat zoom punches (named Accent,
  so `duration_band` passes them); run through the agent service once on generated clips and a click
  track, not yet judged on real footage. Evidence: `engine/footage.test.js` (pace, accents),
  `engine/qualityPolish.test.js` (accent punch).
- **Quiet footage defaults**: Partly verified (plan level). compose {footage} adds no effect without a
  measured reason: downbeat punches only with `accents:true`; one hit, and flash or glitch cuts, only
  on a real drop (6 dB rise), sized by the rise and the shot's brightness; no beat step or slam title;
  push direction and frame blending only from measured footage; the deck never deals loud accents,
  not even as a signature; a plain cut on a song seam counts as designed. Not
  yet rendered on real footage. Evidence: `engine/footage.test.js` (quiet by default),
  `engine/deck.test.js`, `engine/qualityStyle.test.js` (plain_beats).
- **Seam-locked cuts**: Partly verified (plan level). compose {footage} with music reads the song's
  sections and seams (the `.song` file fit_music keeps) and cuts on each seam to the millisecond,
  holds a dropout through to its return or the next section edge, cuts each section at a beat range
  from its loudness (sections under about 4 dB apart stay in the middle range; the pace or
  treatment scales the ranges; a hold of twice the range only over measured motion), and dissolves
  only on smooth passages; a clip too short for its shot cuts on an earlier safe beat, else holds
  its last frame to the seam. The dry-run plan lists the seams it cut on. Synthetic song shape only;
  not rendered on real footage.
  Evidence: `engine/footage.test.js` (seam-locked cuts).
- **Cut snapping and burst**: Partly verified (plan level). Inside a section, compose {footage} moves a cut onto
  a clip's measured impact near a song hit (impact frame on the hit, up to 2 output frames early, never late;
  the impact held for its frames at the clip's measured rate; a plain cut; motion carry only breaks ties), and
  cuts one burst of short shots only over a measured onset-dense run of 1 to 2 bars, on shots within 0.1 luma
  of each other, never in a calm edit (zero is normal). Synthetic hits and impacts only. Evidence: `engine/footage.test.js` (cut snapping and burst).

## Intentional account-free behavior

kino needs no account, signup, login, profile, subscription or billing. Collaboration uses anonymous
sessions and optional display names; sharing uses revocable view, comment and edit links.

- Comment authors are a private per-browser key; mentions send no email.
- Downloads, waveforms and media originals need no account or provider token.
- Sound search (Openverse, Internet Archive) and explore (ListenBrainz genre tags) need no account and send only the search words or tags and kino's user agent.
- Inspire runs `yt-dlp` with `--ignore-config --no-cookies --no-cache-dir` and sends no personal
  details; footage never enters a project.
- Voiceover transcription and cut-out run on this computer; nothing is uploaded.
- Crew agents never receive the owner token on a command line or in their environment.
