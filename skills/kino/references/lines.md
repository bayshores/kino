# Kept clip lines

`keep_line` keeps a said line of a clip (the rest of the clip goes quiet), levels it over the song and shows its subtitle. Find a line with `look source` (sound-library reference).

## Level and mix

- `keep_line` levels a clip line 6 LU over the song (never under -16 LUFS), up to +12 dB, and holds the mix: no dip when a lift alone does it; else the shallowest dip (one that would churn the song shares the nearest earlier dip within 3 s, else the line is lifted); a line still short gets the music out (the note gives the LU short and a dip that keeps the music in). Receipt: `way` (lift, dip, shared or out), `ways` (each way with its factor and headroom, ending with the measured peak and the render's auto gain; `duck_audio` prints it too), the mix vs the 2 s around.
- The mix is measured: a lift past the limiter's -1.5 dBFS ceiling brings the line down (receipt `peak`), and `mix.line`, `ways`, `mix` and the summary give the lowered line. Words over 3 LU off the line's median come back to 3 LU (0.04 s "Line level" ramps; receipt `even` {median, spread, places:[[start, end, dB]]}); `even:false` keeps them as said. A clip under volume 0.5 is evened only with `even:true` (the note gives its spread and places), but the render levels a kept line to its `voiceLevel` while the clip keeps a line (its Line up/Line down marks); a copy without them stays a bed.
- `keep_line {lift:true}` keeps the song untouched and lifts the line within the headroom; the note gives the LU reached and how many LU short of +6. A lift (asked or default) removes an earlier dip under the line unless it also holds another kept line or a heard voice-over (the note names it); inside another line's dip it leaves that dip and the note gives the song as heard and the line's LU over it. A given `dip` or `dropout` sets it aside, with a note. Sent again, it measures the song without earlier dips (and `fit_music`'s fades), so it picks the same dip; with several in one edit, each line's return note is rewritten after all are placed. With the music out, the note says 'is back by <s>' when the dip ends by the song's drop before the planned beat; a dip that joins one under the next sound gives the time the music is back up, not the beat.
- `duck_audio` leaves lines `keep_line` kept as they are (receipt `keptLines`); `replace:true` rebuilds them. After `keep_line` it can still dip on the drop: read the song's volume keys (`inspect`) before trusting 'heard at full'.
- `check {fix:"auto"}` keeps a line `keep_line` leveled at that level and turns the song down instead, never under x0.3; when a kept line alone still holds the peak over, 'not applied' gives the dB left and the line's factor that clears it.

## Music out

- Music out for a line: `{op:"keep_line", layer:"clip", from:5.5, to:7.2, dropout:true}` (and `wait`): the music hits back on the downbeat within wait (default half a bar), else on the next beat; the receipt gives the gap and the wait that reaches the downbeat. A drop or section cue within wait after the last word: the music stays out until it and hits back on it (receipt `drop` {at, cue, before, after, rise}). Lines sharing one music-out dip get a low bed 6 LU under the quietest line, measured (receipt `bed` {at, bed}); `dropout:true` keeps the gap silent. `dip` (0 to 1) sets a fixed dip instead (not both).

## Subtitle

- Subtitles show the words as printed unless `text` is sent: keep_line {layer, line:"<as printed>"}; English: keep_line {layer, from, to, text:"<gloss>"}. For under 2 words or unsure ones the note prints each word with its p: send `text:"<the words said>"`. The subtitle stays inside the line (only its 0.12 s fade runs past the end; its page ends 2 frames before the next kept line or subtitle; receipt `subtitle.at` [first, last]). One already over the line stays when you send no `text` or `name` (`subtitle {name, kept:true}`): a caption group that all comes on during the line, or a track page sharing at least half the heard words; a credit or another voice's track does not stop the line's own. Replace it with `text` and `name:"<it>"` (the line's own group); a kept track page's note gives `update_layer {layer:"<page>-text", properties:{text}}`, so the rest of the track stays.
